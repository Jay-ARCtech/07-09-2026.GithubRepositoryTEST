# Privacy Policy — PARRY CORE

**Last updated:** 20 September 2026

## The short version

PARRY CORE does not collect, transmit, store or share any personal data. It
makes no network requests of any kind. There is no account, no login, no
analytics and no advertising.

## What is stored, and where

The game saves one item on your device:

- Your progress: high scores, best wave, shards, unlocked cores, upgrade
  levels, daily mission progress, and your audio/haptics settings.

It is written to your device's local application storage and never leaves it.
It is not backed up to any cloud service — on Android, automatic backup is
explicitly disabled for this app.

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time from
**Settings → Reset All Progress** inside the game.

## What is not collected

No name, email address or account. No device identifier, advertising ID or
IP address. No location. No contacts, photos, camera or microphone. No usage
analytics, crash reports or telemetry. No cookies or tracking technologies.

## Network access

The app performs no network requests. The Android build ships without the
`INTERNET` permission, so network access is prevented by the operating system
rather than merely avoided by the app.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Vibration (Android) | Haptic feedback on impacts. Can be turned off in Settings |

No other permission is requested on either platform.

## Advertising and purchases

This version of the app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

If a future version adds an optional rewarded video or a one-off purchase, this
policy will be updated before that version ships, and the relevant third-party
provider's own privacy policy will be linked here.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children. It contains no ads, no purchases, no chat and no links out of the
app.

## Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing for us
to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. All data the app creates
is on your device and under your control.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it, and
the updated policy will be published with the release it applies to.
