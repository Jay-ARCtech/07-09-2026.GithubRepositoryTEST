Difficulty

Survival mode offers 12 selectable difficulties, from Easy (fewer enemies, softer hits, a shorter level-up climb, and tougher troops held back far longer) up through Hard, Nightmare, and further flavor difficulties like Clone, Swarm, and Elite Uprising. Pick one from the Difficulty Select screen right after hitting Play. War Mode and the Daily Challenge always run at Hard-equivalent tuning.
